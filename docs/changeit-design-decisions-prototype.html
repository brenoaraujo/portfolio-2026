1. Problem

    76% dropped off at payment

    Change

    Tier and billing on one page

    Why

    The third-party card form sometimes loaded blank, so the flow felt broken even when it worked, and 25% went back to the tiers page. We couldn't change that form, so I designed around it: a clear loading state, the tier kept visible during billing, and a way to change tiers without losing progress.

  2. Problem

    57% left before billing

    Change

    Pick a tier before creating an account

    Why

    We asked people to create an account before they understood the offer or the price. I moved tier selection first, with a fallback for landing pages that couldn't pass a tier, so the ideal path worked without breaking the others.

  3. Problem

    55% chose email, listed last

    Change

    Email and password come first

    Why

    The page led with social login, but most people picked email, and a third of visitors bounced. Putting their choice first and removing a step made the start of sign-up shorter.
